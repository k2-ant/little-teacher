# Changelog

The version number shows in the bottom-right corner of the start screen (for example `v1.7`).

## v2.7 (2026-11-12)
One game for everyone, no modes:
- **The little-one switch is gone.** There is nothing to set up for a younger child.
- **Easier for small hands, for everyone:** tracing is a little more forgiving, the right answer glows straight after a wrong tap, and hints come after 7 seconds instead of 9.
- **Every touch sparkles:** a ring and a burst of twinkles wherever a finger lands, and a small trail behind a dragging finger.
- **A resting hand no longer blocks the game:** if one finger or a palm is down and still, a new touch takes over.

## v2.5.1 (2026-11-12)
Polish, from going through every screen at iPad and phone size:
- **Start screen:** only one teacher now (a second one used to stand by the door behind the class).
- **Star jar:** it stands beside the plant, so ▶ no longer hides its stool on phones; the stars inside are clearer; stars earned while the jar is full wait for the next jar instead of being lost.
- **Stars you can see being earned:** the tracing board and the shape sorter show a little jar, and a star flies into it each time she finishes something there.
- **Tracing board buttons** (back, again, next) are the same chunky blue and green buttons as everywhere else.
- **Night screen:** the "good morning" button has a big smiling sun.
- **Party sticker:** floats up and away when she taps it, instead of sliding to an empty corner.

## v2.5 (2026-11-12)
A sprite pass over the whole game:
- **Softer shading everywhere:** every rounded shape (friends, furniture, decorations, food, stickers) gets a half-tone between its lit side and its shadow, and a small glint.
- **Bigger, glossy eyes** for the friends and the teacher, so faces read from further away.
- **The owl's glasses** are solid round frames now (they were dotted), and her belly feathers are tidy.
- **Sun and balloon stickers** redrawn: a big smiling sun, a shiny balloon with a curly string.
- **Chairs** have rounded backs and a little more detail.

## v2.4 (2026-11-12)
Reasons to come back tomorrow:
- **The star jar (el frasco de estrellas):** a jar on a little stool in the classroom. Finishing something drops a star in: a traced letter or number, a built word, a round of shapes, an answered question, a friend who feels better, the watered plant, attendance, a story, lunch, a whole day. The jar is saved straight away, so a five-minute visit still counts. When it holds 12 stars it glows: tap it for a **class party** (confetti, dancing, party music) and a new sticker.
- **A sticker album:** the sticker book now has a place for all 36 stickers, shown as grey shapes until she earns them. There are 11 new day stickers (apple, watermelon, donut, ladybug, bee, kite, umbrella, cherries, pencil, planet, snowman) and 9 party stickers: one for each friend in the class, then a trophy. Tap a sticker and it jumps. The book opens at her newest sticker.
- **A teacher for each girl:** the pink face button on the start screen asks who is teaching today. Three teachers can share the iPad, each with her own classroom, decorations, stickers, star jar and school days. The classroom that was already there stays with the first teacher, untouched.
- **Dress up the teacher:** three round buttons under her card change her hair (7 colours), her dress (6) and her skin (5). Louise's own look is still the first one.

## v2.3 (2026-11-10)
Clearer letters and art for a pre-reader:
- **School letters:** every letter, number and syllable is now a smooth, round ball-and-stick letter like the ones on her classroom wall (single-storey a and g, a plain l, no slashed zero, every accent and ñ ¡ ¿), drawn sharp at full screen resolution. Tiles show school-paper lines so p, q and g hang and b and d rise. Tracing guides are the same letters.
- **Chunky buttons** that look pressable: outlined, with a lip that presses down. Yellow for activities, green for go, blue for tools.
- **Picture-only activity cards** that bob when it's their turn, and one clear word per Horario card.
- **Big feelings faces:** tears, red cheeks and frowns, wide scared eyes, yawns, on cards tinted with the mood colour.
- **Question cards:** bigger answer pictures, a small grey ✕, and the ✓ no longer covers the friend.
- **A calmer wall** during lessons, a hidden turtle and mouse you can spot, a grey-plum owl and a golden puppy so the browns don't blur, bold hopscotch, and the teacher waves goodbye in front.

## v2.2 (2026-11-10)
Small hands, based on research about how young children use touch screens:
- **Tap, then tap:** in the shape sorter and the centros chart, tap a piece or a face, then tap where it goes. Dragging still works, and shapes now drop in from much further away (and find the matching hole).
- **The decorating tray tucks away** while she places things, so the class and her decorations stay visible. Tap the ▲ or a tab to bring it back.
- **The red card is set apart** from the star and green card, a little smaller, and needs a short press to pick up, so no friend gets one by accident.
- **Right answers echo the word:** the friend says the answer, "¡Eso es!", then the answer again.
- **Bigger touch areas on phones** for the Horario and the buttons.
- **Friends help with hints:** sometimes a friend points and hops toward what to do next, instead of the hand.

## v2.1 (2026-11-10)
From watching a pretend 4-year-old play:
- **Centros you take part in:** each centro waits for the teacher (a raised paw and a glowing sign). Tap one and the teacher walks over for a question about that centro. Every answer leaves a reward there (a painting, an open book, letters, a block tower) and a gold star; all four done means a class cheer and a new round with the bell.
- **Play starts with the class:** the students walk in and Trabajo de la mañana begins. Decorar is still on the Horario any time, and ▶ goes back to where you were.
- **Hints match the screen:** no more "tap the arrow" while drawing on the whiteboard.
- **Tap the teacher** any time: she twirls, waves, or waves to the class and they wave back.
- **Right answers celebrate:** a big ✓, a bouncing card and confetti; the feelings check-in ends with a big heart.
- **Picture-first Horario:** bigger, clearer icons (an ear for Heggerty, a mystery visitor at the door, the teacher with a book…); the current card bounces.
- **Tracing:** a gentle nudge if she stops partway, and tapping the stars counts them.

## v2.0 (2026-11-09)
A rethink so it's easy to understand, for kids and grown-ups.
- **Horario:** the day follows a picture schedule down the left side, modelled on Louise's real classroom chart (Trabajo de la mañana, Calendario, Alfabeto, Recreo, Lectura con Maestra, Heggerty, S.L.A, Centros de Aprendizaje, Almuerzo, Lector Misterioso, Casa). A clothespin marks now; ▶ goes next; tap any card to jump. Each part shows only its own buttons; the free-time menu, 🏠 and the extra corner buttons are gone.
- **Recess outside** on a new playground: slide, swings, sandbox, seesaw, hopscotch, apple tree and ball, following the day's weather.
- **Eight students:** a cat, a bear and a mouse join, each with their own personality, trick, snack and voice. Two rows of four desks.
- **Decorating, redone:** one-tap themes (Océano, Espacio, Jardín, Arcoíris, Otoño, Fiesta, Invierno, Cuentos), tap an item for colour / size / delete bubbles, undo, and 15 new things including an art wall with her whiteboard drawings, a reading tent and a class hamster.
- **Lector Misterioso:** a grandma visits and reads a story.
- **Better voices:** the owl's narration and the new students use ElevenLabs' newer model, recorded at higher quality.

## v1.9 (2026-11-09)
Easier to use:
- The refresh button needs a long press and does nothing offline (before, one tap with no Wi-Fi could break the game until back online).
- New decorations dragged from the tray can't fall into the trash by accident.
- 🏠 asks "¿Ya nos vamos a casa?" before ending the day.
- Tap a star, card or snack, then tap a student (dragging still works). The hint hand shows how.
- Hints everywhere (recess, centros, snack, story), never hidden under the tray, and they suggest ringing the bell.
- One tap brings the turtle out for attendance. Tap to hurry the morning.
- One back arrow (top-left) everywhere, including the whiteboard, sticker book and centros chart.
- Feelings faces are big with their mood icon; questions without a picture show a speaker and repeat themselves.
- Lowercase p, g, y, j, q have tails. Bigger letters and stamps on phones.
More fun:
- ¿Qué tiempo hace hoy? Weather at circle time changes the window, calendar and recess.
- Ayudantes jobs chart: line leader, lights, calendar and plants.
- Everyone raises a paw; repeated taps lead to each student's own trick.
- A crown every 5 stars; an all-green day ends in a dance (extra on viernes); the turtle can be silly too.
- Traced letters fill an alphabet line on the wall.
- She serves snack herself; three story books to choose from.
- Knock knock: a late friend, a surprise package with a new decoration, or a ball for the puppy.
- The class counts number stamps together on the whiteboard.
